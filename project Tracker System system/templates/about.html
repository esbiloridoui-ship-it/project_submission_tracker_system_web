Who We Are

Why we built the Class Project Submission Tracker.

The Class Project Submission Tracker is a class system that keeps every project, due date, and submission status in one place, so nothing gets lost or forgotten. Students can see their projects, due dates, and submission status, while the teacher adds projects and updates records for the whole class.

Our Purpose

What we want to achieve with this system.

🎯

Our Mission

To make tracking class projects simple, clear, and organized for everyone in the class.

👀

Our Vision

A class where students always know what is due and the teacher can see every submission at a glance.

🤝

Our Goal

To reduce missed deadlines and confusion by keeping project records in one shared system.

Who It Is For

The system has two kinds of users, each with their own dashboard.

👩‍🎓

Students

View all your projects with due dates and status. Check what is submitted and what is still pending. Students have view-only access.

👨‍🏫

Teacher

Add projects to students, update submission status, and manage all student records with full access.

Features

Simple tools for both students and teachers.

👩‍🎓

Student View

Students can view all their projects, with due dates and status, in a clear read-only dashboard.

👨‍🏫

Teacher Control

The teacher can add projects, update submission status, and manage all student records.

✅

Submitted & Pending

Projects are sorted into All, Submitted, and Pending tabs so nothing gets missed.

🔐

Secure Login

Each user registers with a School or Employee ID and password to access their own dashboard.
